# Design System: Pace

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Squared Notebook"**

Pace is a school notebook used as a tool: cool squared paper, ballpoint-blue ink and a red margin rule. The ink does every interactive job (the one primary action, links, the switch that is on, the chosen chip, the selection bar, the focus ring, done); the red margin is status only (the now-line on Day, the expected-length mark on the running line, overdue, Urgent, destructive, the waiting count). Everything at rest is neutral paper and graphite text, so the content (task titles, times, the day's shape) is the brightest thing on the screen.

The app works in Operate mode on every surface: the person is in the middle of a task, so scanability, consistency and platform expectations outrank expression. The world contributes type rhythm, palette, density and one signature detail; the shell, navigation and controls are the platform's (a Material navigation bar and ripple on Android, web semantics and a CSS state layer on the web). A screen is one ground with grouped list sections on it; elevation is only for things that float. Time is drawn as time: one formatter, tabular figures, and on Day a rail whose bars line up with the rows they belong to.

The signature lives on Day and in the running card: Day's rail sits on a faint dot grid (only inside the rail), crossed by a red now-line; the running activity reads as a 2 px ink line with a 1 px red mark at its expected length. Colour is never a dot: a project, category or running activity leads with a 32 hue tile (its tint, an icon in its ink). Both themes, both scripts (Latin and Cyrillic) and the worst case (long Russian names, 1 284 tasks, font scale 1.6) are part of the design, not tests after it. With no stored preference and no light OS setting, the page starts dark.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One cool accent (ink blue) for every interactive job; red is status, never decoration.
- One surface with grouped lists; separators only between rows of a group, inset to the text.
- Inter only, thirteen fixed type roles with a phone and a desktop column, tabular figures for every number.
- A 4-pt spacing scale and seven radii; the Tailwind scales are replaced, so nothing off-scale exists.
- Depth by tonal step; the overlay shadow only on floating layers, an inner hairline in dark.
- Every press answers within a frame; every overlay leaves the way it came; nothing exceeds 300 ms, and reduced motion is a 160 ms fade, never nothing.

## Colors

Cool paper neutrals carry the screen; one ink-blue accent and one red margin colour do the talking; a twelve-hue family (plus slate) carries categories and projects as pen colours.

Every role exists in a light (`light-…`) and a dark (`dark-…`) token; components reference the light token and switch to the `dark-` sibling of the same role in dark theme. On the web every role is a `--pace-*` CSS variable set per theme, so one class serves both. The dark theme is ink-night, not a grey inversion. Every colour pair the kit draws is listed in `packages/core/src/design/pairs.ts` and held to its contrast floor by a test.

### Primary
- **Ballpoint Ink** (light #2E4AD6 / dark #97A9FF): the accent. The one filled primary button per view (on desktop the sidebar's New task), the switch track when on, a chosen chip, a chosen calendar day, the 4 px leading bar of the selected row and the current nav item, the check circle at the moment of checking, the running line, the text caret. Its text form **Ink Text** (#2E4AD6 / #ACBAFF) is for links, plain text buttons (Undo «Вернуть», the toast's action) and `status-success` (done is ink, as in a notebook). **Ink Pressed** (#2640BC / #8498F7) is the pressed primary button. **On-Ink** (#FFFFFF / #0B0D14) is text and icons on an ink fill. **Ink Wash** (`accent-quiet`) is the one-time highlight of what an assistant proposal just created, and the usage bars in Settings.
- **Focus Ink** (#2E4AD6 / #ACBAFF): the focus ring, 2 px with a 2 px offset, on every focusable element; a text field draws it on any focus, not only the keyboard's.
- **Selection Ink** (`selection`): web text selection only. A selected row is marked by `fill-1` plus the leading ink bar, never by this wash.

### Secondary
- **Red Margin** (light #C3352B / dark #FF8274, `status-danger`): the now-line on Day, the expected-length mark on the running line, overdue and late times, the Urgent word, destructive actions (label and 2 px border), field errors, the waiting count on Inbox. Its wash (`status-danger-quiet`) is a token with no kit use: the destructive button moved to a bordered cell because the danger label measured 4.31:1 on the wash.

### Tertiary
- **Burnt Orange** (#B04A0C / #FFA870, `status-warn`): "past expected" only: the running line past twice its Expect and the question «Всё ещё занимаетесь?». Wash `status-warn-quiet` for notices.
- **Question Ochre** (#795300 / #F2C275, `status-question`): assistant questions and the «?» mark on a field the model only guessed.
- **Logo Lime** (#5E8C00 on light / #C8F03C on dark, `logo`): the lime chevron of the Pace mark. Used only by the logo, the favicon and the app icon.
- **Hue family** (`hue-<name>-stroke`, `hue-<name>-ink`, plus a `tint` at 12 % light / 18 % dark in the sidecar): red, orange, amber, yellow, lime, green, teal, cyan, blue, indigo, violet, pink, slate. `stroke` draws lines, rail-bar edges and chart marks (≥ 3:1 on `bg-app` in both themes); `ink` is text and icons on its own tint; `tint` is the fill of tags, hue tiles, coloured chips at rest and rail bars. Activity categories: work blue · study violet · task green · food orange · commute yellow · hygiene cyan · rest teal · chores amber · social pink · sport red · sleep indigo · other slate. Projects keep their ten stored names (`coral` is drawn in red).

### Neutral
- **Cool Paper** (#F4F5F9 / #0D0F15, `bg-app`): the screen ground, the page colour and `theme-color` of the web build, the grouped-list background.
- **Sheet White** (#FFFFFF / #161922, `bg-cell`): grouped-list cells, text fields, the running card, a running time-bar tile, the tab bar, the destructive button.
- **Lifted Sheet** (#FFFFFF / #1F2333, `bg-elevated`): sheets, dialogs, popovers, toasts, tooltips, the segmented control's thumb.
- **Margin Paper** (#ECEEF4 / #0A0C11, `bg-sidebar`): the desktop sidebar and the tablet rail.
- **Inset Paper** (#F8F9FC / #12141B, `bg-inset`): a token kept for nested fields; the kit's text field sits on `bg-cell`.
- **Graphite** (#12141C / #ECEEF4, `label-primary`): titles, row titles, values, a control's only icon, the progress fill and the slider.
- **Pencil** (#474C60 / #C3C7D5, `label-secondary`): meta lines, section headers, secondary values, default icon colour, a closed or dimmed task's title, the checked-at-rest disc.
- **Faded Pencil** (#646A80 / #A0A5B9, `label-tertiary`): captions, timestamps, placeholders, the switch's off knob and the segmented thumb's edge.
- **Erased** (#B4B8C7 / #4A4F63, `label-quaternary`): never body text. Disabled labels and glyphs, the sheet handle, skeleton bars, scrollbars, a read-only check.
- **Ruled Edge** (#878CA0 / #767C93, `outline`): the boundary of a control that has no fill to mark it (a text field at rest, the switch track off, the unchecked check circle, a dashed draft field, a gap on Day's rail).
- **State fills** (`fill-1` hover and the selected row, `fill-2` pressed overlay on the web and neutral controls, `fill-3` the current nav pill and the Android ripple, `fill-4` tracks and neutral tags): ink-tinted alpha, so they compose on any layer.
- **Rules** (`separator`, `separator-opaque` where alpha would stack): between rows of a group, inset to the text start; the sidebar and rail edge; the toast's and tooltip's edge; the line above an overlay's footer.
- **Dot Grid** (`grid`): the notebook dots inside Day's rail, nowhere else.
- **Scrim** (`scrim`): behind sheets and dialogs.

### Named Rules
**The One Ink Rule.** Ink blue is the only interactive colour, and a screen at rest shows at most one filled ink element. On desktop the sidebar's New task button is that element, so screens keep their own actions tonal until something is ready to commit.

**The Red Margin Rule.** Red marks status: the now-line, the expected-length mark, overdue, Urgent, destructive, errors, what waits. It is never decoration, never a brand flourish, never a category.

**The Pen Colour Rule.** A coloured thing is its hue's tint under its ink: a project tag, a coloured chip at rest, a hue tile leading a project, category or running activity row, a bar on Day's rail (with a stroke edge, because the tint alone is under 3:1). Colour is never shown as a dot. Importance has no colour of its own: Urgent is a red word, Low (Необязательно) draws the title in `label-secondary` at 400, Normal shows nothing.

**The Logo Lime Rule.** Lime belongs to the Pace mark, favicon and app icon. It never appears as an interface colour.

**Body Font:** Inter
**Label/Mono Font:** Inter with tabular figures; no second family

**Character:** One grotesque for every job, Latin and Cyrillic, set at Material/HIG body sizes with a gentle ≈ 1.15 ratio. Hierarchy comes from size, weight (400, 500, 600, 700) and the four label colours, never from a second face or decoration.

Inter is self-hosted on the web as four variable files (Latin, Latin Ext, Cyrillic, Cyrillic Ext), so Russian is never drawn in a fallback; the phone embeds the TTFs. Frontmatter sizes are the phone values; at ≥ 1024 px every role switches to its desktop column through the same class (desktop values and tracking in the sidecar). On Android sizes are `sp` with font scaling on; chrome (tab labels, badges, time-bar labels) caps at a 1.6 multiplier, content does not. Text yields in a row and declares a policy: wrap (the default, the only one for UI strings), truncate (one line, the full value in the web `title`) or clamp(n).

### Hierarchy
- **Large Title** (700, 32/38 phone, 28/34 desktop, −0.022em): the top-level screen title, at the same height on every destination.
- **Title 1** (600, 24/30, 22/28 desktop): the title of a pushed screen (task, project, preset).
- **Title 2** (600, 20/26, 18/24 desktop): sheet, dialog and detail-pane titles.
- **Headline** (600, 17/22, 15/20 desktop): headings inside content, the running activity's name, «Pace» in the sidebar header.
- **Body** (400, 16/22, 15/21 desktop): row titles, paragraphs, field text (16 on a phone so a browser never zooms). Task-row titles use 500.
- **Callout** (400, 15/20, 14/19 desktop): button labels (at 600), the toast message, empty states, sheet subtitles, nav-item labels.
- **Subhead** (500, 14/19, 13/18 desktop): section headers of grouped lists (sentence case, `label-secondary`), chip and segment labels.
- **Footnote** (400, 13/18, 12/17 desktop): meta lines, field labels, hints and messages, tooltips, key hints, the Expect beside the running time.
- **Caption** (500, 12/16, +0.01em): tags, tab labels, time-bar tile labels, chart labels.
- **Caption 2** (600, 11/14, +0.02em): count badges only. Nothing functional goes below 11 px.
- **Numeric Value** (500, 16/22, 15/21 desktop, tabular): durations, times and counts in rows, and the running activity's time so far.
- **Numeric Stat** (600, 28/32, 24/28 desktop, tabular): Insights tile values.
- **Numeric Live** (600, 22/26, 20/24 desktop, tabular): a token role with no shipped use; the running card gave it up for Numeric Value under the name (owner, 2026-11-10).

### Named Rules
**The No Kicker Rule.** Nothing sits above a title: no eyebrow, no kicker, no small-caps date. The date is a subtitle under the large title (Now, Day) or lives in the period control (Week, History, Insights).

**The Tabular Time Rule.** Every duration, clock and count uses a numeric role or the `numeric` flag (tabular figures) and one core formatter: Russian gets a non-breaking space before units («2 ч 05 мин», «1 284»), a decimal comma, and hours roll into days past 48 h.

**The One Section Header Rule.** A section header is `subhead`, sentence case, `label-secondary`, 24 px above and 8 px below, inset to line up with cell text, announced as a heading of its level, with at most one trailing count or action.

## Layout

A 4-pt scale is the only spacing that exists: the token steps 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64 (the frontmatter keys are the token indices; the Tailwind utilities alias them as `0.5` = 2, `1` = 4, `2` = 8 … `16` = 64, plus named layout steps such as `gutter`, `row-x`, `section`). The Tailwind spacing, size, radius and type scales are replaced, not extended, and arbitrary values are a lint error (`pace/no-raw-design-values`). Sizes are px/dp, so Android and the web measure the same.

**Shell.** One shell, three regions: navigation · content · inspector. `compact` < 600 px (phone: the navigation bar, pushed screens, bottom sheets); `medium` 600–1023 (an 80-wide rail on `bg-sidebar` plus content; overlays are still bottom sheets; rotation unlocked); `expanded` ≥ 1024 (sidebar 248 + content + inspector 400, min 360, max 480; sheets become centred dialogs). The inspector exists only on screens with a selection and collapses when nothing is selected.

**Page template.** One `ScreenHeader` per screen (top-level: large title, an optional subtitle or period control, up to two icon actions; pushed: a 48 bar with Back, context and actions over a `title1`), then content in a column of 720 (lists) or 960 (Day, Insights) at most. Screen gutter 16 phone, 24 in a pane, 32 desktop. Grouped-list rows pad 16 × 12 (16 × 10 desktop), gap 12 between leading slot and text, minimum height 52 (44 desktop); 8 between groups, 24 (28 desktop) between sections; header 8 below the top inset (28 desktop) and 12 (16) above content; sheets pad 20 (24).

**Density.** Calm-dense: rows sized by their content, a two-line clamp on task titles, no decorative panels. Text yields, controls do not: every control keeps its size and the text beside it truncates, clamps or wraps by its declared policy; UI strings wrap and are never cut. Key hints show only at desktop width with a keyboard.

**Region budget.** Fixed bottom chrome (time bar + navigation bar) takes at most 40 % of the viewport height, even with two activities running and the past-expected question asking (asserted in `e2e/time.e2e.ts`); the running card stays compact rather than scrolling.

**Hit targets.** 48 × 48 dp on Android, 44 × 44 px on the web. A smaller visual (a 32 chip, a 24 check, a 32 small button, a tonal icon disc) is centred inside a transparent full-size target that is the layout box, never reached with a negative margin; the overlay and the ring belong to the visual.

### Named Rules
**The One Surface Rule.** A screen is one ground with grouped lists on it. No cards in cards, no hairline around every container, no bordered boxes stacked in a column.

**The Notebook Grid Rule.** The dot grid (a 1 px dot every 12 px in `grid`) exists only inside Day's rail. It never becomes a page background, a card texture or a decoration elsewhere.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is a tonal step. Light goes white-on-paper (cell on app); dark goes lighter-on-darker (cell, then elevated). One shadow token marks things that float: in light a soft two-part drop, in dark a 1 px inner hairline plus a deep drop. Content never floats.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Overlay, light** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(20, 28, 70, 0.14), 0 2px 6px rgba(20, 28, 70, 0.06)`): sheets, dialogs, popovers, toasts, the sync and update banners.
- **Overlay, dark** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(190, 200, 255, 0.08), 0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`): the same layers in dark.
- **Card** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(20, 28, 70, 0.05)` light, `none` dark): a token with no shipped use; the running card is flat.

### Named Rules
**The Float-Only Rule.** Elevation is for things that float (sheets, dialogs, popovers, toasts, banners). Lists, rows, groups, the running card and notices sit flat on their ground. Tooltips float on a hairline, without a shadow.

**The One Boundary Rule.** Content containers mark their edge by fill alone: no border around groups, cells, the running card, chips, tags or tiles. A line appears only where a fill cannot carry the boundary at 3:1: a control without a fill (`outline`), the switch track off, the segmented thumb (`label-tertiary` edge), the destructive button (2 px danger), a rail bar (its stroke), and the edge of a toast or tooltip floating over content.

## Shapes

Soft, consistent corners that nest: `xs` 4 (the selection bar, rail bars), `sm` 8 (small buttons, nav items, tooltips), `md` 12 (buttons, text fields, time-bar tiles, hue tiles, Day's rail, the sidebar's New task), `lg` 16 (grouped-list groups, the running card, popovers, toasts), `xl` 20 (dialogs), `sheet` 28 (bottom-sheet top corners), `pill` (chips, tags, count badges, switches, the segmented track and thumb, the tab and rail pills, progress tracks, icon buttons).

Lines are part of the form language: 1.75 px icon strokes and check rings, the 2 px ink line with its 1 px red mark, the 4 px leading selection bar, the dashed draft border, the red now-line.

### Named Rules
**The Nested Radius Rule.** Inner radius = outer radius − padding, never below `xs`: a 16-radius group with 8 inset holds 8-radius controls.

**The Pill-or-Plate Rule.** Small labels and toggles are pills (chips, tags, badges, switches, segments, indicators); anything that holds a layout (buttons, fields, tiles, groups, cards, overlays) is a rounded rectangle from the scale.

## Components

Every component is built on the kit in `apps/app/src/ui` (`Pressable`, `Text`, `Icon`, `Group`, `ListRow`, `SectionHeader`, `ScreenHeader`, `Disclosure`, `Presence`, `Sheet`, `Dialog`, `Popover`, `Toast`, `Tooltip`, `TextField`, `TimeInput`, `Link`/`NavItem`); screens never use a raw pressable, `Text` or `TextInput`, and never import lucide directly. These are lint errors: the bare-Pressable import ban, `pace/kit-primitives`, `pace/no-raw-design-values`. Icons are lucide through the kit's `Icon`, stroke 1.75 at every size (16 inline, 20 control, 24 bar), `label-secondary` by default, `label-primary` when the icon is the only label, ink only for the primary action; Stop is a filled square. Haptics on Android only: check-off and destructive confirm Confirm, start/stop/switch Clock_Tick, menu long-press Long_Press, refusal Reject.

**State layer (every pressable).** On Android the platform ripple in `fill-3` over the content (borderless on icon buttons and the check). On the web a CSS overlay on `::after`, as round as the control: hover `fill-1` only for a hovering fine pointer, pressed `fill-2` in 0 ms and out in 100 ms on the exit curve, a row with a control beside its press area tints as one object. Focus-visible: the ink ring 2 px + 2 px offset on the control or its visual. Selected/checked: role-specific, never colour alone. Disabled: label `label-quaternary`, fill `fill-1`, never opacity. Forced colours map boundaries to `ButtonBorder`, the ring and the checked state to `Highlight`, and drop the overlay.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (`md`, 12); 48 high on a phone, 40 drawn on a desktop inside the 44 target; side padding 20 (16 desktop). `small` is 32 with `sm` (8) corners and 12 padding. Label `callout` 600 that wraps, never cut; optional leading 20 icon; a trailing key hint (Alt+N style) only at desktop width with a keyboard.
- **Primary:** ink fill, On-Ink label; pressed Ink Pressed. One per view.
- **Secondary:** tonal `fill-2`, Graphite label, no hairline (Stop, «Да, ещё»).
- **Plain:** no fill, Ink Text label (Undo «Вернуть», Cancel). **Plain danger:** the same in Red Margin, for a removal History can bring back.
- **Destructive:** `bg-cell` with a 2 px Red Margin border and a Red Margin label, for a step that cannot be undone (Wipe data, Delete account).
- **Hover / Focus:** the state layer; busy swaps the icon for a spinner in the label's colour and keeps the label, so the width never jumps, and focus survives the busy spell. No scale on press.
- **Width:** hugs its label from 1024 px, fills below for a screen's main action; inside a sheet buttons fill; a flush text button pulls back by its padding to align with the text above.
- **Icon button:** a round full-target control, glyph in `label-primary`; a toggle shows a `fill-3` disc and `aria-pressed`; `tonal` draws a 32 `fill-2` disc; an unnamed icon button names itself in a tooltip.

### Chips
- **Style:** a 32 pill (40 when tall) inside a full target, `fill-2`, `label-secondary`, `subhead` label, max width 180 with an ellipsis; a chip whose words must show whole (a date, an estimate) takes its row's width. A project or category chip at rest is its hue's tint under its ink. No outlines, no dots.
- **State:** chosen = the solid Ballpoint Ink fill with On-Ink label and icon (the ink wash measured 1.1:1 against the cell). Filter chips are checkboxes, choice chips radios in a roving group, picker chips say `aria-expanded`.

### Tags and hue tiles
- **Tag:** a 20-high pill, 8 px side padding, `caption` label; hue `tint` fill + hue `ink` text, or `fill-4` + `label-secondary` without a hue. Not interactive; it yields and truncates before it pushes its row.
- **Hue tile:** a 32 square in `md` corners, the hue's tint with a 16 icon in its ink (`fill-4` for no colour). It leads a project, category or running-activity row; decorative, the name beside it is the text.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** groups and the running card `lg` (16).
- **Background:** `bg-cell` on `bg-app`.
- **Shadow Strategy:** flat (see Elevation).
- **Border:** none; rows inside a group are divided by `separator`, inset to the text start; a labelled edge-to-edge separator may split a plain list (Now: tasks with a deadline, then without).
- **Internal Padding:** rows 16 × 12; the running card 12 × 8.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 48 (phone) / 44 (desktop) high, `md` corners, 16 side padding, `body` text, `label-tertiary` placeholder, `bg-cell` with a 1 px Ruled Edge border; a visible `footnote` label above (a real `<label for>` on the web), 8 between label, field and hint. Multiline starts at 96.
- **Focus:** the 2 px Focus Ink ring 2 px outside the field, drawn by the field itself on any focus; the browser outline is removed.
- **Error / Disabled:** error = Red Margin border + a `footnote` message with an alert glyph under the field, only after blur or a submit attempt; disabled = `fill-1`, `label-quaternary` text, no border.
- **Draft:** text the assistant is still reading shows the same field with a **dashed** border.
- **Slider (web):** a 4 high `fill-4` track filled in `label-primary`, a 24 `label-primary` thumb in a 2 px `bg-cell` ring.

### Selection controls
- **Switch:** a 52 × 32 pill. On: ink track, 24 On-Ink knob. Off: `fill-2` track in a 2 px Ruled Edge ring, 16 `label-tertiary` knob. Disabled: `fill-1` track, `label-quaternary` knob. A switch row flips from anywhere on the row.
- **Segmented:** a `fill-2` pill track for two to four choices; the chosen segment is a `bg-elevated` pill with a 1 px `label-tertiary` edge and a 600 label.
- **Check circle:** a 24 circle with a 1.75 Ruled Edge ring in a full target. It fills on press-in (ink disc + On-Ink check) with the Confirm haptic; checked at rest it is a `label-secondary` disc with a check in the page colour, and the title goes `label-secondary` with a strikethrough, so a Done list is never a column of ink. Read-only (History "as it was"): a `label-quaternary` ring.
- **Progress bar:** a 4 high `fill-4` pill track; the fill in `label-primary` (task progress), a hue stroke (an activity) or Burnt Orange (past the Expect).
- **Count badge:** a 20 pill hugging its digits (min 20, 8 padding, `caption2` 600, "99+" past 99): danger = Red Margin with cell-coloured digits for what waits; neutral = `fill-3` with secondary digits for what is unread. It hangs off a glyph's top-trailing corner and grows outward.

### Navigation
- **Phone (compact):** the navigation bar on `bg-cell`, 64 high plus the inset, four equal destinations (Now · Day · Inbox · More; Week, Projects, Insights, History and Settings live behind More). Current: a 64 × 32 `fill-3` pill behind the 24 icon in `label-primary`, the `caption` label in `label-primary` 600; others in `label-secondary`. Labels wrap rather than cut; chrome caps font scale at 1.6. On the web it is a list of links.
- **Desktop (expanded):** a 248 sidebar on `bg-sidebar` with a `separator` edge: the mark, «Pace» (`headline`) and the bell (no profile name); New task as the shell's one ink button (44 high, `md`, `callout` 500, its key hint); then eight 44-high nav items (`sm`, 12 padding, 20 icon, `callout` label, a trailing danger badge on Inbox). Current: `fill-3`, `label-primary` 600, the 4 px leading ink bar and `aria-current="page"`; a screen it owns but does not name keeps the bar and weight without the fill.
- **Medium:** an 80-wide rail on `bg-sidebar`, a 48 `fill-2` New task tile, then icon-over-`caption` items with the same 64 × 32 pill.
- **Selected row:** `fill-1` under the row and a 4 px ink bar (`xs`) at its leading edge, inset 8 top and bottom.

### Time bar and running card (signature)
- **Time bar tiles:** four fixed tiles (Calendar, Task, Chores, Misc «Прочее»), each a quarter of the row, 56 high, `md` corners, a 20 icon over a one-line `caption` label. At rest: `fill-2`, icon and label `label-secondary`. Running its activity: `bg-cell`, icon and label `label-primary`, label 600, and a 4 px bar in the category's stroke along the bottom. The tiles stay visible while something runs; only the typed field folds.
- **Running card:** `bg-cell`, `lg`, 12 × 8 padding, not a live region. Row one: the hue tile, the name in `headline` (truncated before Stop), Stop (a secondary button with the filled square). Under the name: the time so far in Numeric Value and the Expect in `footnote`; past twice the Expect the warn question takes that line, «Да, ещё» stands before Stop and Stop becomes its icon. Then the ink line: a 2 px `fill-4` track filled in ink to now with a 1 px red mark at the expected length, the fill turning Burnt Orange past it; it moves with the minute tick. An activity alongside is one compact row (name, time, a tonal Stop icon) under a separator.

### Day timeline (signature)
- An editable list with a 32-wide rail at its leading edge (`md` corners, the dot grid inside). Beside each row a bar at that row's measured top and height: 12 wide in the category's tint with a 1.75 stroke edge (`xs`), a parallel activity a narrower bar beside it, a gap a dashed `outline` bar. The 1 px red now-line crosses the row that holds now and a running bar ends on it. The rail is hidden from screen readers; the rows carry the times.

### Overlays
- **Sheet (compact and medium):** `bg-elevated`, `sheet` top corners, 20 side padding, a drag handle, a `title2` title and on the web a close cross; the footer sits under a separator. It travels its own height in (280 enter curve) and out (200 exit curve) while the scrim fades in place; a drag past 30 % of its height or a flick ≥ 800 dp/s dismisses it. No Back or Cancel button.
- **Dialog (expanded):** centred, 560 (forms) or 400 (confirmations), at most 85 % of the window height, `xl` corners, 24 padding, a close cross; scales 0.96 → 1 with a fade in 220 and out to 0.98 in 160.
- **Popover / Menu:** anchored below its trigger (above when there is no room), 8 from the anchor and the window edge, 360 wide (280–400), `lg` corners, 8 vertical padding; 0.98 → 1 with a fade from the anchor's edge, 160 in, 110 out.
- **Toast:** `bg-elevated`, `lg`, a 1 px `separator` edge and the overlay shadow; the `callout` message wraps whole (data quoted in it is cut to 48 graphemes), at most one plain ink action and a close button; max 480 wide; shown 5–10 s by length, held while pointed at or focused. It rises 8 px with a fade in (220) and drops 4 px with a fade out (160).
- **Tooltip (web):** `bg-elevated`, `sm`, 1 px `separator` edge, 12 × 8 padding, `footnote`, max 280; after 400 ms hover, at once on keyboard focus; hoverable, Escape dismisses; never on tap.
- **Skeleton:** `label-quaternary` pill bars in the final layout's shapes, shown only after 300 ms of waiting, breathing to 0.7 opacity and back over a 1.2 s period (600 each way); still under reduced motion.

### Motion
Durations 100 (press, hover) · 160 (fades, popover, presence fade/lift, row enter) · 220 (dialog, toast, presence rise/scale/slide, close-up) · 280 (sheet, web route push); exits 70 · 110 · 160 · 200. Curves: `enter` cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), `exit` cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1), `move` cubic-bezier(.3,0,0,1) for in-place change. A checked row's content fades out in 160 on the exit curve while its box closes up to nothing in 220 on the move curve, so the rows below rise continuously; the next step runs from the animation's completion, never a timer. Presence presets: fade; lift (4 up); rise (8 up, the toast's enter); drop (4, the toast's exit); scale (0.96, a dialog); slide (16 from the trailing edge, the inspector). On the web a pushed route slides 12 px in and fades over 280, and the screen revealed by a pop comes back from the other side over 200; tab switches have no transition. Reduced motion replaces every travel and scale with a 160 ms fade, never nothing (a checked row fades and then closes at once; a route fades). Springs: sheet drag release (damping 30, stiffness 320), thumbs (damping 24, stiffness 420).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep ink blue (#2E4AD6 / #97A9FF) as the only interactive colour, with at most one filled ink element per screen at rest.
- **Do** reserve Red Margin (#C3352B / #FF8274) for the now-line, the expected-length mark, overdue, Urgent, destructive, errors and what waits.
- **Do** draw a coloured thing as its hue's tint under its ink: a pill tag, a coloured chip at rest, a 32 hue tile leading its row.
- **Do** put every number through a numeric role (tabular figures) and the core formatter, with non-breaking spaces in Russian.
- **Do** build screens as one `bg-app` ground with `bg-cell` groups (`lg`, 16) and inset separators.
- **Do** build every control on the kit's `Pressable` so it gets the one state layer: `fill-1` hover, `fill-3` ripple / `fill-2` overlay, a 2 px + 2 px ink focus ring, `label-quaternary` + `fill-1` when disabled.
- **Do** add every new colour pair to `pairs.ts` and check every decision in both themes, in Russian, at 320 / 390 / 1440 and at font scale 1.6.
- **Do** pair every overlay's enter with its exit, stay at or under 300 ms, chain the next step on completion, and give each a 160 ms reduced-motion fade.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put an eyebrow, kicker or small label above a title.
- **Don't** use red as decoration, as a category colour or as a brand accent.
- **Don't** use the logo lime (#5E8C00 / #C8F03C) anywhere but the logo, favicon and app icon.
- **Don't** show "Normal" importance, or give importance its own colour beyond the red Urgent word.
- **Don't** nest cards in cards, outline every container, or add a border where the fill already holds 3:1.
- **Don't** put shadows on content; the overlay shadow belongs to sheets, dialogs, popovers, toasts and banners.
- **Don't** use text glyphs (↵, ⋯) as icons, an outlined square for Stop, or a pulsing running dot.
- **Don't** show colour as a dot; a coloured thing is a tint under its ink.
- **Don't** spread the dot grid beyond Day's rail.
- **Don't** use off-scale spacing, radii, font sizes or arbitrary values, a bare pressable, or a direct lucide import; lint rejects them.
- **Don't** dim disabled controls with opacity, or show state by colour alone.
- **Don't** animate list mounts, filter changes, tab switches or day switches, or pair a fixed timer with an animation.
