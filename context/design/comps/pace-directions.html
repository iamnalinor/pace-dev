Pace — three directions

STOP 1 of the design overhaul. The same two screens, built three ways: Now on a phone (dark and light) and Day on a desktop (light), with the Russian lived-in data the app is tested on. The shell, navigation and controls are identical in every frame on purpose; a direction only changes type rhythm, palette, density and one signature detail. Day's blocks are sample data shaped like the owner's real morning.

Rendered in HTML, not in the app: proportions and colours are exact, interaction is not. Frame text is Inter with tabular figures for every time and count.
